First Post

May 23, 2026 · 2 min · Rainbain Blog

Hello, welcome to my blog!

I am an electrical engineer and embedded systems engineer from Florida. I started this blog because despite my 6-7 years of experience, I don’t have much of a portfolio to look back on. My professional work has always been deep under NDAs and a lot of my personal projects have been undocumented one off learning experiences.

I want to change that, and share both my one off projects, and parts of my more long term personal projects.

I usually take on all sorts of projects that have landed me from writing an Open Source SDK for the Wii, to a variety of other projects I wish to document.

I specialize in the oddities of electrical engineering and digital logic with a deep interest in radio communications and graphics processing units. I work on a mix of power systems, communications, and FPGA. I also have a deep computer science background and have made multiple FreeRTOS and Linux ports, as well as some work within LLVM and Mesa here and there.

In most of my work I favor simplicity and low power consumption over bleeding edge. I find it’s usually much harder to refine designs into seemingly simple and reproducible designs than it is to just scale up and hope for more power.

I hope you find the projects I post here in the future as interesting as I find them or at the very least educational.
